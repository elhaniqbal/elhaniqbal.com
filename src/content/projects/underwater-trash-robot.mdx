## The problem

Underwater trash is hard to detect, localize, and collect. Visibility is poor. The platform needs to be stable enough to maneuver precisely while carrying a retrieval mechanism. And everything has to work in the real water, not just in the lab.

## What we built

Trash-E is an RC floating platform with a crane-line collection mechanism and a depth camera for underwater perception. The goal was to detect trash-like objects underwater, localize them, and retrieve them with the physical crane mechanism.

The system had to handle water, imperfect visibility, platform stability, and the unpredictable behavior of a real retrieval mechanism under load. It also had to work well enough on demo day to win a national competition, which it did.

## What actually happened at competition

Technically, the prototype had issues. Hardware prototypes always do. The crane mechanism had opinions about when it wanted to cooperate. The depth camera had opinions about visibility conditions. The platform had opinions about current.

We won anyway, because the concept was strong, the integration demonstrated genuine engineering ambition, and we were honest about the tradeoffs during the judging. That experience taught me something real: being clear about what works and what does not, and why, is a more credible engineering position than pretending everything is perfect.

## Recognition

The project won the CSME 2025 National Design Competition and was featured by MindFuel.ca as part of their recognition of innovative student engineering work. You can read about it on their site if you want more detail.

## What it showed me

This project was valuable because physical prototypes teach things that software projects do not. The feedback is immediate and unambiguous. If the mechanism jams, it jams. If the perception fails, you can see it fail in real time. There is no hiding behind "it works on my machine."

It also reinforced that cross-disciplinary engineering is genuinely harder than single-domain work. Getting the mechanical, electrical, and software pieces to cooperate under real conditions, on a timeline, with a team, is the kind of problem that does not have a clean solution.

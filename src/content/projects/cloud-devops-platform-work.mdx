## What this represents

This case study is intentionally written at a higher level because some professional work cannot be described with internal details. The important signal is that I have worked in cloud DevOps/platform contexts where infrastructure changes affect real systems and teams.

## Skills developed

- Terraform and infrastructure-as-code workflows;
- cloud networking concepts such as VPCs, subnets, firewall rules, and gateways;
- IAM and least-privilege access control;
- CI/CD pipelines and deployment discipline;
- documentation for engineers and operators;
- debugging platform issues under real constraints.

## Why it matters

A lot of new grads can build a local app. Fewer can explain how that app should be deployed, secured, monitored, documented, and maintained by a team. This experience strengthened my ability to think beyond code and into operational reality.

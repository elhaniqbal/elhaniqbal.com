## The project

The Ukebox was a second-year engineering design studio project built for IGEN 230. The brief: design something functional, present it, defend the decisions. We landed on a ukulele-jukebox crossover — a physical device with mechanical and electrical components that combined the form factor of a jukebox with a ukulele-based musical mechanism.

## What it taught me

Early design projects are valuable in a specific way: you do not know enough yet to be afraid of the problem. You just build the thing, find out what does not work, and fix it. The poster session forced us to explain why we made certain tradeoffs, which turned out to be harder than making the tradeoffs in the first place.

IAM is not just a permissions table. It is the control plane for who can change your system.

When I evaluate an IAM design, I ask six questions:

1. Who is the identity?
2. What action can it perform?
3. Which resource can it touch?
4. Under what conditions?
5. What is the blast radius if it is compromised?
6. Can we audit what happened later?

This framework works across GCP, AWS, Kubernetes RBAC, GitHub Actions, service accounts, and internal platform tooling.

## Why this matters

Many infrastructure incidents are not caused by one dramatic bug. They come from overly broad permissions, unclear ownership, weak environment separation, and credentials that outlive their purpose.

A good platform engineer designs access so that the safe path is the easy path.
